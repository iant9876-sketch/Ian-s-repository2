# Lamplight

A small browser game. You keep the lamp at Gull Rock Light. Ships are sailing blind toward the shoals, and you sweep the beam across the fog to warn them off.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs no build step and no install.

- **Aim:** move the mouse, drag on a touchscreen, or use ← →
- **Focus:** hold the mouse button, Space, or the on-screen Focus button. The narrow beam signals a ship faster, but it covers less sea.
- Hold a ship in the light until its signal ring fills. It then turns for harbour.
- A ship saved close to the rocks counts double.
- Three wrecks end the watch.
- **M** toggles sound.
