# Theater of War

An AI war simulation. Pick a war, or describe any war you like, then command it in plain words. Claude acts as the game master: it decides what actually happens, moves the other powers, and updates the map, the news and your inbox.

## What's in it

- **Orders and Talk.** Orders take a turn and time passes. Talk lets you speak with your own advisors or foreign leaders without using a turn; deals you strike carry into the next turn.
- **Real-world map.** Wars on the real Earth are drawn on an actual map with territory shading, front lines and troop-movement arrows. Fantasy and invented worlds get a generated map.
- **History.** In historical wars the other powers follow their real goals and timeline unless your choices change the circumstances.
- **The Wire.** Headlines from many different news outlets each turn, plus posts from people inside the world.
- **Inbox.** Regular reports you've asked for, and letters, cables and demands from other leaders. Reply to any of them.
- **Campaigns.** Progress saves after every turn.

## Playing

The game is built to run as a Claude artifact. Claude writes every turn, so the AI features only work when the page is opened inside Claude. Opened on its own in a browser, `index.html` loads and shows the scenarios, but it can't play turns.

Ready-made wars: *Iron Winter, 1983* (Cold War gone hot), *The Sundered Crown* (fantasy) and *Archipelago War, 2041* (near future).
