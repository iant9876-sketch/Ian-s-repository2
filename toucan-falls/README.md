# The Long Way to Toucan Falls

A 3D browser adventure. You play a sloth crossing a bright tropical island to visit your friend the toucan. Climb, swim, and keep an eye on the screen edges: a white flash means a predator is stalking you.

## Swinging on vines

Some trees are joined by a mossy branch with a long vine hanging from it. Many of these vines go straight across the path, from the left side to the right.

1. Climb the tree until you're near the top.
2. Push toward the other tree. The sloth hooks the vine and swings across in a big arc.
3. She lands on the far tree. If that tree has another vine too, push toward the next tree to keep swinging.

Nothing can catch you while you swing.

## Hanging vines and treetop trails

The original vines are back too. These are strung straight between two trees. Climb to where the vine is tied, push toward it, and the sloth hangs upside down and crosses hand over hand.

Some stretches of the trail are treetop trails, with trees zigzagging along both sides. Hanging vines run along each side of the trail, and swinging vines cross it, so you can travel a long way without touching the ground. When you finally climb down, the game tells you how far you went.

## Controls on a tree

- **Up:** climb up. If the tree has a swinging vine ahead, she climbs to it and swings.
- **Left or right:** grab a hanging vine on that side. If there isn't one, she circles around the trunk.
- **Down:** climb down and hop off.
- Turning the camera also makes her scoot around the trunk to the side you're looking at.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. It works on phones and tablets as well as computers. It needs no install, but it loads three.js from a CDN, so it needs an internet connection.
