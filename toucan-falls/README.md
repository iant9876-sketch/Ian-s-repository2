# The Long Way to Toucan Falls

A 3D browser adventure. You play a sloth crossing a bright tropical island to visit your friend the toucan. Climb, swim, and keep an eye on the screen edges: a white flash means a predator is stalking you.

## Swinging on vines

Some trees are joined by a mossy branch with a long vine hanging from it. Many of these vines go straight across the path, from the left side to the right.

1. Climb the tree until you're near the top.
2. Push toward the other tree. The sloth hooks the vine and swings across in a big arc.
3. She lands on the far tree. If that tree has another vine too, push toward the next tree to keep swinging.

Nothing can catch you while you swing.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. It works on phones and tablets as well as computers. It needs no install, but it loads three.js from a CDN, so it needs an internet connection.
